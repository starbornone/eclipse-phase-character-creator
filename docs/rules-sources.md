Posthuman Studios licenses Eclipse Phase releases under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 unless a release says otherwise. The November 2023 *Eclipse Phase Second Edition Quick-Start Rules*, v2.4, carries that licence. The publisher asks for credit to Posthuman Studios, LLC and a link to its site. For the Quick-Start profiles, store the published health values and their source page. The numbers describe those examples and omit NPC threat pools, equipment, and rules prose. Data lives in [the versioned sample dataset](../src/rules/datasets/sample-morphs.ts). Existing drafts retain their legacy morph names and placeholder durability. The check calculation uses the verified rule for all drafts.
